Metrics by model

Scene score
The reference label describes the whole scene, so each model is scored once per scene on its captions from every camera combined.
Precision
Of the meaningful words the model wrote, how many also appear in the reference label. Low means it said a lot the label doesn't mention.
Recall
Of the meaningful words in the reference label, how many the model mentioned. Low means it missed what the label says.
F1
One overall score combining precision and recall (higher is better; green ≥ 50%, amber ≥ 25%, red below).
Avg words
Average caption length, for context only; it isn't scored.

Words are compared after removing filler words (a, the, on…) and folding plurals. Synonyms don't match, so scores are low in general. Compare models with each other, not against 100%.
